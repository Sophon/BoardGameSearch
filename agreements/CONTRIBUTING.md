# Contributing to BoardGameSearch

Thanks for your interest in contributing.

## Contribution license (inbound = outbound)

By submitting a contribution to this project — via pull request, patch, or any
other means — you agree that your contribution is licensed under the same terms
as the project itself: the **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0** (see the
`LICENSE` file) together with the additional terms in
[`NOTICE.md`](./NOTICE.md).

You confirm that:

- you have the right to submit the contribution and to license it under these
  terms; and
- you grant the project maintainer the same copyright and patent licenses over
  your contribution that the project's license grants to everyone.

This is a lightweight "inbound = outbound" policy (no separate CLA to sign). It
keeps the project's licensing consistent and means contributions can be
relicensed or dual-licensed by the copyright holder in the future without
having to track down every past contributor.

## How to contribute

- Open an issue to discuss significant changes before starting.
- Keep pull requests focused and reasonably small.
- Match the existing code style and conventions.
